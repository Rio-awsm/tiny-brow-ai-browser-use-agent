Chandrayaan-3

Mission typeLunar lander, rover
OperatorISRO
Launch siteSatish Dhawan Space Centre
Landing siteLunar south pole region

Chandrayaan-3 is the third mission in the Chandrayaan programme, a series of lunar exploration missions developed by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO). It consists of a lander named Vikram and a rover named Pragyan, similar to those aboard Chandrayaan-2.

Background

After the crash of Vikram in 2019, ISRO proposed a repeat mission. The Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency was later partnered on LUPEX.

Objectives

  • To demonstrate a safe and soft landing
  • To demonstrate rover roving on the Moon
  • To conduct in-situ scientific experiments

Design

The lander carries ChaSTE, ILSA and RAMBHA-LP. The propulsion module carries SHAPE. The rover carries APXS and LIBS.

Launch

Launched on 14 July 2023 on an LVM3 from Sriharikota. It entered lunar orbit on 5 August 2023.

Landing

The lander touched down on 23 August 2023, making India the fourth country to soft-land on the Moon. The site was named Statio Shiv Shakti.

Results

Sulphur was confirmed near the pole. See also Aditya-L1 and Gaganyaan.
