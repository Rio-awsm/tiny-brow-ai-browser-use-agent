Chandrayaan-3

Chandrayaan-3 is the third lunar exploration mission under the Indian Space Research Organisation's (ISRO) Chandrayaan programme. It consists of a lander named Vikram and a rover named Pragyan, similar to those aboard Chandrayaan-2. Launched on 14 July 2023, the lander touched down near the lunar south pole on 23 August 2023, making India the fourth country to achieve a soft landing on the Moon and the first to do so near the south pole.
